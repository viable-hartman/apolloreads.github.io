    🧠 Read Together¶

    Reading together for just 15 minutes a day builds vocabulary, focus and a lifelong love of stories.

    • Turn on captions (CC). Reading along with spoken words is one of the best ways to build fluency.
    • Read the title out loud together and talk about what you expect.
    • Pause the video and let your child read the next line before the narrator does.
    • Point out sight words (the, and, said, was) each time they appear.
    • Ask: “Who was the main character? What problem did they have? How was it solved?”
    • Have your child read one page aloud to you — or to a pet or stuffed animal audience!
    • Who was your favorite character in The Cat and the Hat, There's No Place Like Space, and why?
    • What was the problem in the story? How did it get solved?
    • If you could change the ending, what would happen instead?
    • How do you think the main character felt at the end?
